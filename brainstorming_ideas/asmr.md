# Nature

- Fall colors
- Ponds, lakes, and reservoirs
- Creeks and Rivers - especially rapids
  - Ideas for composition - beginnings, middles, and endings:
    - Move from downstream to up:
      - *Beginning:* start downstream, pan to upstream rapids
      - *Transition:* move upstream to rapids
      - *Middle:* study the rapids:
        - zoom in,
        - pan across them,
        - end this main part with slo-mos: slow, slower, slowest, slower, slow, back to normal speed
      - *Transition:* move further upstream from rapids
      - *Ending:* pan from rapids to further upstream
    - Move from upstream to down:
      - *Beginning:* start upstream, pan to downstream rapids
      - *Transition:* move downstream to rapids
      - *Middle:* study the rapids:
        - zoom in,
        - pan across them,
        - end this main part with slo-mos: slow, slower, slowest, slower, slow, back to normal speed
      - *Transition:* move further downstream from rapids
      - *Ending:* pan from rapids to further downstream
