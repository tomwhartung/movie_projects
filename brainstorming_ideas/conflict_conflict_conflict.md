
# conflict_conflict_conflict.md

In the Great Courses series of lectures Screenwriting 101, neurologist turned screenwriter Angus Fletcher
maintains the key to entertaining audiences is "**conflict, conflict, conflict**".

Ideally, this conflict is between the main character and "the entire story world". [p. 32]
