# Movie Projects

This section contains the Big Picture of the Big Picture.

Putting this down in words should help:

- Free up room in my brain for actually working on the projects

# Goals

The goals here are to have several projects that increase in complexity, giving me the skills I need to make viewers,
and ultimately actual paying customers, happy - and thereby eventually maybe make some actual money.

## List of Specific Projects

- 1. Nature Videos
  - Videos of *Fall Colors* and *Falling Water* that I took when I first got my new camera
  - The music for these videos should be ambient, harmonious, relaxing, very quiet, and chill
- 1. Protest Videos
  - Videos of ongoing protests against the lawless, narcissitic, authoritarian and fascist Trump administration
  - The music for these videos should be very disharmonious, loud, in-your-face, and agressive
- 1. How-to Videos
  - Videos that may be of interest to a broad audience, but are targeted to **movie makers** who want to add their own music
  - The music for these will be fairly minimal, and to be determined by the nature of what the video is about
- 1. Days in the Lives Videos
  - Videos about a specific person, company, or group of people
  - The music for these will be original, custom, and one-of-a-kind, to be determined by the person or group the video is about
